Riding With Us

Creator standards. The Moto Legion.

This is not a club. Nobody owns your name, your content, or your bike. There are no dues, no colors to earn, and no chain of command. The Moto Legion is a formation of southeast Michigan creators who take the work seriously and pull each other up.

What that means in practice: we cross-promote, we ride together, we share what works. Nobody carries anybody. Everyone here got their own house in order first, and that is the whole filter.

The standard below exists to build you up. It is the base layer that makes growth stick: with those pieces in place, every ride you film and every follower you earn compounds instead of leaking away. Clear the bar for your own sake first. The crew is what you get on top of it.

The Standard

Five things we look at. All of them, not most of them.

  1. The Brand

    Your house is in order. Concretely:

    • A brand worth remembering. Thought through and unmistakably yours. Not a template, not a copy of somebody else's, and not a name you picked in five minutes. Something people can recognize on sight and repeat from memory.
    • One identity everywhere. Same handle spelled the same way, a real logo that holds up on a shirt or a thumbnail, and the same colors and fonts from your site to your socials.
    • A website of your own. Your own domain, not a link-in-bio page.
    • Every social claimed. Handles locked down even on platforms you do not post to yet, so nobody else takes them.
    • Profiles filled out. Real bio, real photo, real links on every account you actually post on.
    • Established, not empty. A posting history that shows you have been at this, not an account opened last week.

  2. The Riding

    You can ride, and you actually do. Proficient on the bike and putting real miles on the road, not filming the same parking lot every week. This is a riding crew before it is a content crew.

  3. The Content

    Interesting, well made, and worth someone's time. Polished enough that you would put your name on it in front of strangers, because you are. It has to be a positive for the motorcycle community, not another clip that gives riders a bad name.

  4. The Discord

    You link The Moto Legion Discord from your platforms and use it as the hub where your followers gather. One room for all of our audiences is what makes the whole thing work. Rides get planned there, and that is where the crew actually lives.

  5. The Philosophy

    Same direction as the rest of us: building motorcycle culture in southeast Michigan and giving the scene something to be proud of. You are here to grow it, not to farm it. That also means no baggage that lands on everyone else, because we put our names next to each other and that trust has to be earned and kept.

How It Works

There is no application form and no vote.

Get in the Discord, ride with us, and put in the work on your own brand. If the standard above describes you, it will be obvious to everyone without a pitch. If it does not yet, the list is the roadmap. Come build it and come back.
